What Thrift does

Automatic capture

Every new bank SMS becomes a logged expense the instant it arrives. Scan your inbox once to pull in transaction history from any date — no manual entry, ever.

A queue that doesn't guess

Low-confidence matches and every incoming deposit wait for your say before they count as spending — income is never auto-recorded.

Budgets that hold

Per-category caps and one overall monthly budget, with alerts at 80% and 100%. Past months freeze into read-only history, so nothing you look back on ever shifts.

Vacation mode

Trip spending tracks apart from the monthly budget, attributed by date rather than stamped at entry — edit a transaction's date later and it self-corrects.

The dashboard

Everything spent, at a glance

Total spend by month, a category breakdown, and what's still waiting for review — the numbers you'd otherwise dig for across a dozen bank SMS, already assembled.
